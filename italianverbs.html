Base verbs

Learn the base verb and you've learned its family — every member conjugates the same way, just with a prefix (venire → intervenire, prevenire…). These two buttons are filters: they combine with Travel, Regular/Irregular and Essere/Avere (and the tenses you pick) as an intersection. Tapping a single base below adds or removes its family — only the members that pass the active filters.
